How does open-book auto sourcing work in Ontario?

Open-book auto sourcing lets you buy work trucks and cargo vans straight from closed dealer-only auctions at the real wholesale price. You pay the exact price on the auction invoice plus a flat sourcing fee ($1,500–$2,995, depending on vehicle type) — no dealer lot markup. This typically saves contractors several thousand dollars per vehicle; you can see the exact number for your target vehicle on our Savings Calculator.

WHOLESALE VS. RETAIL

See the Spread on Every Unit
