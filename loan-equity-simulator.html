Ontario HST & Open-Loan Notes

GST/HST-registered businesses buying a vehicle for commercial use may be able to claim some or all of the HST paid back as an Input Tax Credit, subject to CRA's business-use percentage rules and the passenger-vehicle capital cost limits under CCA Class 10.1 — this is not automatic or unlimited, and depends on your specific situation. Similarly, "open loan, no prepayment penalty" is a common feature but not a universal one — confirm it's actually in your contract before counting on it. We are not accountants, tax advisors, or lenders; confirm your specific numbers with your CPA and financing provider.
