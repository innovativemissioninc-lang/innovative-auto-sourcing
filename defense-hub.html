GTA Dealership Quote & Fee Auditor

Under Ontario's Motor Vehicle Dealers Act, a registered dealer's advertised price already has to include freight/PDI, admin and documentation fees, the OMVIC transaction fee, and any pre-installed product the dealer requires. TOOL 3 · INSURANCE RISK

GTA High-Theft & Insurance Risk Lookup

The GTA has one of the highest vehicle theft rates in Canada, and insurers price that risk into your premium based on the exact segment and trim you drive. This is a directional guide, not a quote — theft-risk ratings are model- and trim-specific and shift year to year, so always confirm the real number with your own insurer before you buy.

Source: Équité Association's 2024 national theft data (the most recent full report available at time of writing) and general industry reporting on keyless-entry relay-theft trends in Ontario and Quebec. Figures are not GTA-specific and this tool does not quote exact dollar surcharges or municipal tracking requirements — read the full picture in our theft & insurance guide, then get a real quote from your insurer for your exact make, model, and trim.
