FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Straight Answers, No Fine Print Surprises.

Everything you need to know about our sourcing fees, deposits, financing, safety inspections, auction arbitration, and how the wholesale dealer-resale model actually works.

How does Innovative Auto Sourcing's wholesale sourcing model actually work?
Innovative Auto Sourcing acquires your exact spec into our own dealer inventory at closed, dealer-only wholesale auctions (OPENLANE, Manheim, EBlock). We are the buyer of record on the auction transaction. Once the vehicle passes inspection and reconditioning, we resell it to you in a separate, disclosed transaction at auction cost plus our flat sourcing fee, with the full auction invoice and every receipt shared open-book. We do not bid as your agent or broker, and you never touch the auction directly.
How much does wholesale auto sourcing cost?
The flat sourcing fee is $1,500 CAD for core passenger vehicles, $2,250 CAD for commercial vans and heavy-duty work trucks, and $2,995 CAD for a white-glove turnkey handover. On top of that you pay the exact raw auction hammer price shown on the invoice, tiered auction buyer fees, a DriveON safety inspection, a post-sale inspection, and any reconditioning at cost. We never add a dealer lot markup.
What is the $500 deposit for, and is it refundable?
The $500 deposit locks in your sourcing parameters — make, model, budget, mileage limit — and is applied directly toward your sourcing fee once your vehicle is acquired. Refund terms for a deposit are set out in your sourcing agreement at the time you submit a request; ask your sourcing coordinator for the current terms before you pay.
Do you offer financing?
Clients can choose to pay by cash or bank draft, or through a third-party prime financing option offered at checkout. Financing is provided and underwritten by a third-party lender, not by Innovative Auto Sourcing — rate, term, and approval are determined by that lender based on your application. We are not a licensed lender or financial advisor, so confirm exact rates and terms directly with the financing partner before you commit.
Are sourced vehicles safety inspected before I take delivery?
Yes. Every vehicle goes through a licensed Ontario DriveON safety inspection and a post-sale inspection (PSI) before commercial plates are transferred. Any repairs identified are completed at wholesale shop cost, with every receipt shared directly with you.
What happens if a vehicle fails inspection or has an undisclosed defect after purchase?
Each wholesale auction platform has its own arbitration policy and timeline — these are not identical across OPENLANE, Manheim, and EBlock, and a failed inspection does not automatically cancel a sale. A qualifying issue is submitted to the auction's arbitration process, which determines the resolution: repair, price adjustment, or in some cases a cancelled sale. We walk you through the specific claim window and process for the platform your vehicle came from at the time of sale.
Is glass etching or anti-theft etching mandatory?
No. Anti-theft glass etching is an optional, dealer-added add-on you can choose at checkout — it is not a government requirement and is never applied without your consent.
What are the ServiceOntario registration and plate fees?
ServiceOntario charges a $32 permit transfer fee if you are moving your own existing plates to the new vehicle, or $17 if you already hold valid plates being re-registered, or $59 for brand-new plates (which already includes the permit fee). The exact fee depends on which of these scenarios applies to you, and we confirm the correct figure before registration.
Which auction platforms do you source from?
We source from licensed, dealer-only wholesale auction platforms in Canada, primarily OPENLANE, Manheim, and EBlock. These platforms are contractually restricted to licensed dealers buying for resale, which is why the vehicle is acquired into our own inventory rather than sold directly to a retail client at auction.
Is Innovative Auto Sourcing OMVIC registered?
Innovative Auto Sourcing Incorporated's OMVIC General Dealer (Used) registration is in progress. The company is not yet trading retail vehicles, and all current wholesale acquisitions are conducted through licensed dealer-only auction channels in accordance with applicable OMVIC Code of Ethics and Consumer Protection Ontario guidelines.
Is the sourcing fee or vehicle purchase tax deductible for my business?
Possibly, depending on your business use of the vehicle. Under current CRA rules, passenger vehicles fall under Capital Cost Allowance Class 10.1, with a 2026 capital cost ceiling of $39,000 before tax, a 30% declining-balance rate, and the half-year rule applying in the year of acquisition. Commercial vehicles like cargo vans and heavy-duty work trucks may fall under different CCA classes. We are not accountants or tax advisors — confirm your specific deduction eligibility with a licensed CPA before you file.
What vehicle types can you source?
Sedans, hatchbacks, and compact/mid-size SUVs under Core Passenger Sourcing; cargo vans such as the Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster and heavy-duty pickups such as the F-250/F-350, Silverado 2500/3500HD, and Ram 2500/3500 under Commercial Van & Heavy-Duty Work Truck Sourcing; and premium SUVs or executive vehicles under our White Glove Turnkey Handover tier.
How long does the sourcing process take?
Timelines vary with vehicle availability at auction, but most orders move from parameter intake to safety-checked handover within a few weeks. Your sourcing coordinator will give you a specific estimate once your target spec and budget are confirmed.
Which areas do you serve?
We serve the Greater Toronto Area, including Toronto, Etobicoke, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, and Oakville, with turnkey delivery available across the region.
