Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Innovative Auto Sourcing Incorporated ("Innovative Auto Sourcing", "we", "us") is an Ontario company. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our sourcing service, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Short version: we collect what we need to understand and fulfil your vehicle request. We never sell your information, and we only share it with the parties needed to complete your purchase, with your knowledge.

1. Who is responsible

Innovative Auto Sourcing Incorporated, Greater Toronto Area, Ontario. Privacy questions: email sourcing@innovativeautosourcing.ca or call (416) 938-1933.

2. What we collect

When you request sourcing or contact us

  • Your name, company name (if any), phone number and email address
  • The vehicle you're looking for: category, make and model, budget, mileage limit, and any notes you add
  • Anything else you tell us by email, phone or text

The sourcing request form on this website opens a pre-filled email in your own email app; nothing is stored on this website. We receive the information when you send that email.

If you buy a vehicle through us

To complete a vehicle sale and register it, we will need more information, such as your address, driver's licence details, insurance information, and payment and (if you choose financing) financing details. We will tell you what is needed, and why, at that point. We only collect these once we are able to sell vehicles (see our OMVIC status).

When you visit this website

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It loads page styles and icons from third-party content networks (Tailwind CSS and unpkg), so your browser connects to their servers, and our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security. The savings and loan calculators run entirely in your browser; what you type into them is not sent to us.

3. Why we use it

  • To understand your request, reply to you and source a vehicle that matches it
  • To prepare your invoice, complete the sale, and arrange safety inspection, registration and delivery
  • To meet our legal obligations, including dealer record-keeping and tax rules
  • To send you updates about your request. We will only send marketing emails if you agree, and every one will let you unsubscribe.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the service:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting (Netlify) and our email provider
  • Parties to your purchase, where needed: the inspection and reconditioning shop, ServiceOntario for registration, your insurer, a transport company for delivery, and a lender if you choose to apply for financing (only with your consent)
  • Regulators and authorities where the law requires it, such as the Ontario Motor Vehicle Industry Council (OMVIC) or the Canada Revenue Agency

Vehicles are bought at auction in our own name, so your personal information is not given to the auction houses.

Information stored outside Canada. Some of our service providers store data on servers in the United States or other countries, where it may be accessible under those countries' laws.

5. How long we keep it

  • Requests that don't lead to a purchase: up to 24 months, then deleted
  • Sale and transaction records: as long as required by law, including dealer record-keeping rules under Ontario's Motor Vehicle Dealers Act, 2002 and Canadian tax law

6. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw consent (for example to marketing emails), or ask us to delete what we no longer need to keep. Email sourcing@innovativeautosourcing.ca. We will respond within 30 days, free of charge.

7. Security

This website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we keep customer records in access-controlled accounts. No system is perfectly secure, so we collect only what we need.

8. Children

Our services are for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes and complaints

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

See also our Terms of Use.
