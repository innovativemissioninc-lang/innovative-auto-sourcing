THE OPEN-BOOK PROCUREMENT PIPELINE

Four Steps From Parameters To Plates.

Every acquisition follows the same transparent, itemized pipeline — from your first intake call to a road-ready, DriveON certified handover in the GTA.

01

Parameter Intake & Deposit

$500 DEPOSIT

You submit your target make/model, budget ceiling, and mileage threshold. A refundable $500 deposit locks in your sourcing parameters and is fully applied toward the flat $1,500 sourcing fee.

02

Closed-Lane Proxy Bidding

AUCTION COST

We bid on your behalf inside licensed dealer-only auction platforms — OPENLANE, Manheim, and EBlock — auditing every condition report and CarFax/CarProof history before a single bid is placed.

03

DriveON Safety & Certified Reconditioning

$250 + $1,000–$1,500 AT COST

Every unit passes a DriveON commercial safety inspection and undergoes certified reconditioning — performed strictly at cost, with every invoice and receipt shared directly with you.

04

Turnkey Handover

TURNKEY DELIVERY

We handle commercial registration, plate transfer, and final delivery — you receive a road-ready vehicle with a fully itemized invoice, anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area.
