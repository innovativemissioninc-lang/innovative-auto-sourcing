THE OPEN-BOOK PROCUREMENT PIPELINE

4 Easy Steps to Get Your Vehicle On the Road.

Every order follows the same simple, transparent process — from your first call to a safety-checked, road-ready handover in the GTA.

01

Tell Us What You Need

$500 DEPOSIT

Give us your make, model, budget, and mileage limit. A refundable $500 deposit locks in your order and goes straight toward your sourcing fee ($1,500–$2,995, based on vehicle tier).

02

We Acquire It at Private Dealer Auctions

AUCTION COST

We purchase your spec into our own dealer inventory inside licensed dealer-only auctions — OPENLANE, Manheim, and EBlock — checking every history report and condition record before we buy, then resell it to you in a separate, disclosed transaction.

03

Safety Inspection & Repairs At Cost

$160 + AT-COST RECON

Every vehicle passes a DriveON government safety inspection and gets any needed repairs — billed strictly at cost, with every receipt shared directly with you.

04

Plates On, Ready To Drive

TURNKEY DELIVERY

We handle registration, plate transfer, and delivery — you get a road-ready vehicle with a fully itemized invoice, anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area.

How do we make sure your vehicle is safe and job-ready?

Every vehicle goes to a licensed DriveON testing centre for an official Ontario government safety inspection ($160), plus a post-sale inspection (PSI) to catch anything the auction listing missed. If brakes, tires, or lights need work, we complete the repairs at wholesale shop cost — with the receipt shared directly with you — before your commercial plates go on.
